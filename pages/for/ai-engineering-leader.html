How I lead

  • Publish the bar. The Standard states what every engineer in the program is trained to, in public.
  • Measure the model, then ship. A non-deterministic step gets an eval and a written target before anyone builds advice on top of it.
  • Make the right thing automatic. Commit format, type checks, tests and security scans are enforced by tooling, so review time goes to design.
  • Give the credit away. Contributors own what they build, under their own names.
  • Write the decision down. A tradeoff that is not recorded gets made again.
