How I work with data

  • Label the source on the row. Salary records carry a dataSource field, so a reader can tell a live Lightcast figure from a Census estimate or a curated value.
  • Validate at the boundary. Generated records must match a schema before they are written. A quote that is not in the source text is rejected.
  • Refuse stale data. Postings older than 14 days and O*NET data older than 30 are rejected, and scripts stop when a live source is down.
  • One definition per number. Each published outcome statistic is defined once, with its source and date, and pinned by a test.
  • Make runs resumable. Batches are small and merge into existing output, so a failed run continues where it stopped.

The reasoning behind each of these is in the decision records.
