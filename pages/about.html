A working engineer who runs a nonprofit

Jerome Hardaway is a senior data and AI engineer and engineering leader. He is the Founder and Executive Director of Vets Who Code, is based in Atlanta, Georgia, and works remotely.

I served in the United States Air Force Security Forces from 2004 to 2009. After I got out I taught myself to write software, and in 2014 I founded Vets Who Code.

Vets Who Code is a fully remote, open-source program that trains veterans and military spouses to become software engineers. It charges no tuition. More than 300 people have come through it, with a 97% job placement rate and more than $20M in collective alumni earnings.

I still write most of the code. In the last twelve months I made 310 of the 361 commits to the organization’s main application. I also built its AI teaching backend and published the agent tooling we use to run the organization. I also write the curriculum standards, mentor the people in the program and teach publicly.
